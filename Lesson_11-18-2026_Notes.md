## 3. 🔧 6 Gentle Italian-to-English Phrasing Polishes

| # | Topic | In the Moment (What You Said) | Natural Native Polish | Why / Rule |
| :-: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Duration: *For* vs. *Since*** (`00:15`) | *"I have been experiencing **since one week** a continuous whistle... it's becoming very **struggling**."* | *"I've been experiencing a continuous ringing (tinnitus) **for a week**, so it's becoming really **exhausting**."* | Use **for** + duration (*for a week / for two years*) and **since** + starting point (*since September*). People *struggle*; situations are **exhausting** or **tough**. |
| **2** | **Helping Verbs & Idiom** (`01:00`) | *"Talking helps me **in avoiding about avoiding** thinking about this whistle."* | *"Talking **helps me avoid** thinking about this ringing — it **takes my mind off it**."* | After *help (someone)*, use the bare verb (**helps me avoid**). Native speakers also love **"it takes my mind off it"** (*mi distrae*). |
| **3** | **Transitioning Levels** (`01:26`) | *"Change from **passing through** conversational English to a more structured English."* | *"Move **from** conversational English **to** more structured, articulate English (or **move beyond** conversational English)."* | Italian *passare da... a...* translates naturally as **move from X to Y** or **transition from X to Y**. |
| **4** | **Employee Well-Being** (`11:11`) | *"The **awareness of employers** is very important... every Wednesday **launch** could give an **ad advantage**."* | *"Employee **well-being** is a top priority... free pizza every Wednesday at **lunchtime** would boost our company's reputation."* | **Employers** = *datori di lavoro*; **employees** = *dipendenti*. In business English, use **employee well-being** or **workplace wellness**. |
| **5** | **Sentence Flow** (`12:43`) | *"About **the flowing of the sentence**. Because I was thinking while I was talking."* | *"I was conscious of **the flow of my sentences**, because I was **thinking on my feet** while speaking."* | Use the noun **flow** (*the flow of my sentences*), and the C1 idiom **thinking on my feet** (*pensare sul momento mentre si parla*). |
| **6** | **Vernacular Upgrade: *Stuff*** (`20:15`) | *"We're going to work at the office on Friday because we have **some stuff to do**."* | *"We're coming into the office on Friday because we have **a heavy workload / a lot of key tasks**."* | Replacing vague fillers like **stuff** with **a heavy workload** or **key tasks** immediately elevates your professional authority. |
