# Lesson 01 Plan — Serena A (Onboarding & Diagnostic)
**Date:** Wednesday, November 18, 2026 (50 min) | **Coach:** Lewis | **Student:** Serena A  
**Goal / Context:** Diagnostic Lesson 1 — Needs discovery, baseline speaking sample, 3-upgrade live demo, and a clear 4-week roadmap.  
**Pacing Rules:** **70% Student / 30% Lewis** | **60-Second Teacher Story Cap** (keep teacher talk crisp; let Serena speak 35+ minutes).

---

## 00–05 min | Warm Welcome & Rapid Rapport
- **Audio & Comfort Check:** Confirm mic/audio clarity and set expectations (*"Today is 70% you speaking so I can tailor every future lesson to your exact goals."*).
- **1-Question Icebreaker:** *"Where are you calling from today, and what's one thing you're looking forward to this week?"*

## 05–15 min | Structured Needs Discovery & Goal Mapping
*Ask 4 targeted open questions; take concise notes in Silent Scribe:*
1. **Context:** *"What does a typical week look like for you—work, study, or daily life—and where does English show up?"*
2. **High-Stakes Moments:** *"Which specific situation makes you feel most pressured in English (meetings, presentations, small talk, interviews, travel)?"*
3. **Friction Points:** *"When you speak, what feels hardest: finding the right words fast, grammar accuracy, pronunciation, or confidence?"*
4. **Target:** *"If we look 3 months ahead, what would make you say, 'These lessons really worked for me'?"*

## 15–35 min | Baseline Speaking Sample (Past • Present • Future)
*Uninterrupted 2–3 minute turns while Lewis listens and logs 3 high-leverage upgrade opportunities:*
- **Prompt 1 (Career / Study Arc):** *"Tell me about your background: what did you do in the past, what are you focused on right now, and where do you want to be in a year or two?"*
- **Prompt 2 (Domain Mini-Scenario):** *"Walk me through a recent challenge you solved (at work or in life) and how you explained it to someone else."*
- **Prompt 3 (Opinion / Culture):** *"What's something people often misunderstand about your city, industry, or culture?"*

## 35–42 min | 3-Upgrade Live Demo
*Show Serena how your feedback system works immediately—positive, practical, zero overwhelm:*
1. **Celebrate 2 Natural Strengths** from her baseline sample (exact quotes).
2. **Deliver 3 High-Impact Upgrades** (*"In the moment you said X → A native speaker would say Y"*).
3. **Live Repeat:** Have Serena say each upgraded sentence aloud once in her own words.

## 42–50 min | 4-Week Goal Roadmap & Micro-Homework
- **42–47 min (Roadmap Agreement):** Confirm lesson cadence, core focus (e.g., vocabulary precision, filler reduction, or structured storytelling), and how *Silent Scribe* notes will be shared after each class.
- **47–50 min (Micro-Homework — Under 5 Min):** Send a **60–90 second voice note** (or 4 bullet points) introducing a topic you care about using **Past • Present • Future**, deliberately using **1** of today's 3 upgrades.
